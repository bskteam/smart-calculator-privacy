Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 29, 2026
Developer: SYMO

Smart Calculator respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android application.

Information Collection

Smart Calculator does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Local Data Storage

The application may store calculation history, favorites, preferences, and cached currency exchange information locally on your device.

Depending on Android backup settings, this data may also be included in system backup and restore services.

Currency Converter

The Currency Converter retrieves exchange rates from the Frankfurter service over HTTPS. Currency codes are transmitted to request exchange rates. Amounts entered by users are calculated locally and are not included in exchange-rate requests.

The external service may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy practices.

Advertising

Smart Calculator version 1.0 does not contain advertising SDKs or display third-party advertisements.

Analytics

Version 1.0 does not include third-party analytics SDKs.

Data Sharing

SYMO does not sell personal information through Smart Calculator. External network communication is used to retrieve currency exchange information.

Children's Privacy

Smart Calculator is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed at children.

Security

Local application information is stored using Android application-private storage mechanisms. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when application features, third-party services, or data practices change. The updated policy will display a revised date.
